# Shop

:::info summary

After reading this, you can buy entitlements and certain hardware products.

:::

In the `Shop`, PosDealers buy fiskaltrust dealer package products on stock to use later in a rollout, and some hardware products. For the products, see [Products & Services](./products/overview.md).

## Entitlements

As a PosDealer, you either pay directly during a rollout or buy **Entitlements** for products on stock. Entitlements are claims to the products, which you later transfer to the outlets of your PosOperators. You buy entitlements in `Shop` > `Products`, and you find your existing entitlements in `Shop` > `Entitlements`.

Your entitlements are used when you roll out products and when you renew subscriptions with existing entitlements. For more information, see [Rollout management](./rollout-plans.md) and [Manual renewals](./subscription-management.md#manual-renewals).

## Buy products and entitlements

1. In the Portal, open `Shop` > `Products` in the left sidebar.
2. Choose the product or entitlement, enter the quantity and click its basket button. The product is added to the `Basket`.
3. Open the `Basket` in one of these ways, then click `Checkout`:
   - In the left sidebar, open `Shop` > `Basket`.
   - Click the `Shopping Cart` icon in the upper-right corner.
4. Check the products, quantities and prices before placing the order.
5. Submit the binding order. The Portal displays an order confirmation and sends you a copy by e-mail.

## Check orders, invoices and entitlements

### Orders

To check the status of your orders, open `Shop` > `Orders` in the left sidebar.

![Orders page in the fiskaltrust.Portal shop listing the placed orders with their status](./images/orders.png)

*Figure 1. The `Orders` page.*

### Invoices

To find the invoices of your orders, open `Shop` > `Invoices` in the left sidebar.

![Invoices page in the fiskaltrust.Portal shop listing the invoices](./images/invoices.png)

*Figure 2. The `Invoices` page.*

### Your entitlements

After an order is processed, the entitlements you bought are listed in `Shop` > `Entitlements`. To see the history of your purchases, click `History`. There, you can also search for an order number.

![Entitlements page in the fiskaltrust.Portal shop listing the purchased entitlements with the History button](./images/entitlements.png)

*Figure 3. The `Entitlements` page.*
