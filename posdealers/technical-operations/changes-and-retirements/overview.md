# Overview - Changes and Retirements

:::info summary

After reading this, you know which upcoming changes to fiskaltrust services may need action on your side, and by when.

:::

## Introduction

This section lists changes to fiskaltrust services that may require you to update your POS systems, network settings or configuration, for example when a TLS version is retired or IP addresses change.

Each notice states:

- which services and environments the change applies to;
- the key dates;
- how to find out whether you are affected;
- what to do, and by when.
