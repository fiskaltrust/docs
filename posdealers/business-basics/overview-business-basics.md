## Introduction

fiskaltrust provides a solution for the checkout and receipt processes of POS Systems. As a PosDealer, you use it to offer your customers (PosOperators) Compliance-as-a-Service and add-on products.

## Prerequisites

Before you use fiskaltrust at a PosOperator, make sure that:

* A **PosCreator** has integrated the PosSystem in the fiskaltrust.Portal.
* The **master data** of both the PosDealer and the PosOperator in the fiskaltrust.Portal is complete and matches their registration with the tax authorities. See [Master Data](../getting-started/operator-onboarding/master-data.md).

:::tip

Practice the first steps in the [Sandbox](../getting-started/sandbox.md) before working in the live system. Follow [My First Cashbox](../getting-started/my-first-cashbox.md) for a guided setup.

:::
