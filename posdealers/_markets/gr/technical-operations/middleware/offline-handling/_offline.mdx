- **Internet:** the Middleware runs in the fiskaltrust cloud and transmits every document to myDATA in real time. Every location needs a reliable internet connection.
- **Middleware not reachable:** the POS system issues the receipts with a notice and sends them to the Middleware without delay once the connection is back. The Middleware transmits them to myDATA as transmission failure.
- **Handwritten receipts:** after a complete outage, the receipts are recovered with the series and number of the paper document.

For the POS system integration, see [Offline Handling (GR)](../../../../../../poscreators/middleware-doc/middleware-gr/cash-register-integration/offline-handling.md).
