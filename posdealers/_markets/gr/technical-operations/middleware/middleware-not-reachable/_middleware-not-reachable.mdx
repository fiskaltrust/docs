- **Connection:** the Middleware runs in the fiskaltrust cloud and is reached over the internet connection of the location. Every location needs a reliable internet connection.
- **During the failure:** the POS system issues receipts with a notice and buffers them.
- **Afterwards:** the POS system sends the receipts to the Middleware without delay. The Middleware transmits them to myDATA as transmission failure.

For the POS system integration, see [Middleware Not Reachable (GR)](../../../../../../poscreators/middleware-doc/middleware-gr/cash-register-integration/middleware-not-reachable.md).
