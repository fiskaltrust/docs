### ChaîneCloud

ChaîneCloud is fiskaltrust's SaaS Middleware platform. It offers the same features as a locally hosted Middleware, but you do not install or maintain any software. Your POS system needs a working Internet connection to communicate with ChaîneCloud over an encrypted **HTTPS** connection.

For more information, see [fiskaltrust products and prices in France](https://fiskaltrust.fr/en/products-prices/).
