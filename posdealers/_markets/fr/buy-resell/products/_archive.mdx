### ArchiveAuditable

ArchiveAuditable is a **cloud service** that provides a searchable, secure and **audit-proof** database for Middleware receipts. When you activate it for a CashBox, its Middleware regularly pushes new receipts into the archive, where they are stored revision-safe. Following French regulations, the data is kept for **at least six years**.

#### Exports

You can access and export the stored receipt data at any time in the fiskaltrust.Portal, for example for a tax audit.

For more information, see [fiskaltrust products and prices in France](https://fiskaltrust.fr/en/products-prices/).
