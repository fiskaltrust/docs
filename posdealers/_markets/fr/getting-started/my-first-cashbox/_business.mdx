The Business Rollout acquires entitlements, transfers them to the PosOperator's outlets, activates them and creates the backend components for the CashBox. In this example, the result is a fully operational CashBox.

This example uses a fiskaltrust.ChaîneCloud plan, which creates a ChaîneCloud. To keep the tutorial short, you roll out the ChaîneCloud to your own account and outlet instead of to a PosOperator.

:::caution

The outlet you select in step 7 must have a **valid SIRET code** configured before you create the CashBox.

:::

1. Sign in to the [Sandbox Portal](https://portal-sandbox.fiskaltrust.fr).
2. In the left-hand navigation menu, click `Rollout management`.
3. On the Manage Products page, `Rollout Products` card, click `Choose`.
4. On the `Choose Rollout Product` page, on the `Business Rollout` card (Rolling out products), click `Choose`.
5. On the `Select Product` page, search for `ChaîneCloud`.
6. On plan **4652-0005**, click `Choose`.
7. On the `Select PosOperators` page, select your own account, marked `My Account`, and click `Rollout 1 PosOperators`.
8. On the `Select Outlets/Queues` page, select the outlet where you want to roll out the product and click `Rollout 1 outlets`.
9. On the `Summary` page, check the plan and the outlet, enter a name under `Define a name for the quote`, and click `Create quote & go to basket`. Creating the quote takes a few seconds.
10. In the shopping cart, verify that the items match the selected products and click `Checkout`.
11. Click `Order bindingly` to complete the order.

The order is listed under `Shop` > `Orders`. Completing the order can take a few minutes. After that, the following items are active in your account:

| Item | Location in the Portal |
|---|---|
| A new Queue | `Configuration` > `Queues` |
| A new CashBox | `Configuration` > `CashBoxes` |
| A new SCU | `Configuration` > `Signature creation unit` |
| A new demo subscription | `Shop` > `Subscriptions` |

*Table 1. Items created by the Business Rollout.*
