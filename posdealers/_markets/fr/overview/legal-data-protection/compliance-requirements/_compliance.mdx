
## Consequences of operating a non-compliant system

French law requires businesses subject to VAT to use accounting, management or point-of-sale software that meets the conditions of inalterability, security, retention and archiving, and to be able to prove it with a certificate or a publisher's attestation.

Failing to produce that certificate or attestation carries a fine of **€7,500 per piece of accounting or management software, or per point-of-sale system concerned**. The business then has sixty days to bring the system into compliance. If that deadline passes without compliance being established, the fine can be imposed again.

The obligation and the penalty are set out in the [loi n° 2015-1785 du 29 décembre 2015 de finances pour 2016](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/eli/loi/2015/12/29/FCPX1519907L/jo#JORFARTI000031732968) and in the Code général des impôts.

Note that the fine is counted per system, so a PosOperator running several tills is exposed several times over.
