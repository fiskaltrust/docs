- **Connection:** with the fiskaltrust certificate, the Middleware runs in the fiskaltrust cloud and is reached over the internet connection of the location.
- **No late signing:** while the Middleware cannot be reached, documents are issued manually on pre-printed forms from a printing company authorized by the AT. Make sure the merchant keeps a stock of forms for every document type that is issued.
- **Afterwards:** every paper document is entered in the POS system and issued by the Middleware in a dedicated manual series.

For the POS system integration, see [Middleware Not Reachable (PT)](../../../../../../poscreators/middleware-doc/middleware-pt/cash-register-integration/middleware-not-reachable.md).
