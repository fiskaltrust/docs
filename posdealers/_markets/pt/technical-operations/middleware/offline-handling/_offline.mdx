- **Internet:** with the fiskaltrust certificate, the Middleware runs in the fiskaltrust cloud. Every location needs a reliable internet connection.
- **No late signing:** while the Middleware cannot be reached, documents are issued manually on pre-printed forms from a printing company authorized by the AT. Make sure the merchant keeps a stock of forms for every document type that is issued.
- **Recovery:** after the outage, every paper document is entered in the POS system and issued by the Middleware in a dedicated manual series.
