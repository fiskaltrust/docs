The Middleware can be used as the fully hosted *CloudCashbox* or installed by you. For a self-installed Middleware, there are two options:

* as local system service on your [POS System or dedicated machine](../../../../../technical-operations/middleware/supported-environments.md)
* as Docker image for [installation in your data center](https://github.com/fiskaltrust/product-de-bring-your-own-datacenter)
