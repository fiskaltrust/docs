This example uses a fiskaltrust.Carefree plan. The plan creates a Carefree bundle, including an OnlineSignature SCU, and a CloudCashbox for immediate use. To keep the tutorial short, you roll out the bundle to your own account and outlet instead of to a PosOperator.

1. Sign in to the [Sandbox Portal](https://portal-sandbox.fiskaltrust.at).
2. In the left-hand navigation menu, click `Rollout management`.
3. On the `Business Rollout` card (`Rolling out products`), click `Choose`.
4. On the `Select Product` page, search for `CloudCashbox`.
5. On plan **4154-0005**, click `Choose`.
6. On the `Select PosOperators` page, select your own account, marked `My Account`, and click `Rollout 1 PosOperators`.
7. On the `Select Outlet` page, select the outlet where you want to roll out the product and click `Rollout 1 outlets`.
8. On the `Summary` page, check the plan and the outlet, enter a name under `Define a name for the quote`, and click `Create quote & go to basket`. Creating the quote takes a few seconds.
9. In the shopping cart, verify that the items match the selected products and click `Checkout`.
10. Click `Order bindingly` to complete the order.

The order is listed under `Shop` > `Orders`. Completing the order can take a few minutes. After that, the following items are active in your account:

| Item | Location in the Portal |
|---|---|
| A new Queue | `Configuration` > `Queues` |
| A new CashBox | `Configuration` > `CashBoxes` |
| Two new demo subscriptions | `Shop` > `Subscriptions` |

*Table 1. Items created by the Business Rollout.*
