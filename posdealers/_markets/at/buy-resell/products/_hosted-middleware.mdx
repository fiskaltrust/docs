### SignatureCloud

SignatureCloud is fiskaltrust's SaaS Middleware platform. It offers the same features as a locally hosted Middleware, but you do not install or maintain any software. Your POS system needs a working Internet connection to communicate with SignatureCloud over an encrypted **HTTPS** connection.

For more information, see [fiskaltrust fiscalization products in Austria](https://www.fiskaltrust.at/products/fiscalization/).
