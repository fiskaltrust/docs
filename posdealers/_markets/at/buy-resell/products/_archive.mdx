### fiskaltrust.POS-Archiv

The fiskaltrust.POS-Archiv is a **cloud service** that provides a searchable, secure and **audit-proof** database for Middleware receipts. When you activate it for a CashBox, its Middleware regularly pushes new receipts into the archive, where they are stored revision-safe. Following Austrian regulations, the data is kept for **at least seven years**.

### DEP exports

You can access and export the stored receipt data at any time in the fiskaltrust.Portal, for example for a tax audit:

- in the **format required by the tax regulations**, the DEP (Datenerfassungsprotokoll)
- in a **complete data export format** that contains all requests and responses sent

For more information, see [fiskaltrust services in Austria](https://www.fiskaltrust.at/products/services/).
