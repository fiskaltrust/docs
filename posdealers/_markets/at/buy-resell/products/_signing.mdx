### Middleware

#### fiskaltrust.RK-Online

fiskaltrust.RK-Online is a yearly, subscription-based online service for signing receipts with the Middleware.

#### a-trust a.sign RK Chip Smart Card

The a-trust a.sign RK Chip Smart Card is a smart-card-based solution for signing receipts with the Middleware.

#### fiskaltrust.HSM

The fiskaltrust.HSM products provide signing based on a hardware security module (HSM) for different performance scenarios, on a yearly subscription basis, for signing receipts with the Middleware.

For more information, see [fiskaltrust fiscalization products in Austria](https://www.fiskaltrust.at/products/fiscalization/).
