### FinanzOnline

FinanzOnline provides electronic access to the tax authorities for entrepreneurs and their representatives, including the registration of signature creation units, and notifications and checks for cash registers, for example the start, annual and stop receipts. For more information, see [FinanzOnline management](../../../../buy-resell/products/3rd-party/finanzonline-management.md).
