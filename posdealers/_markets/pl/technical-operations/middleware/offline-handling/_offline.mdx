- **No sales without a working register:** under Art. 111(3) of the Polish VAT Act, sales must not continue while the register cannot be reached. The POS system stops selling and shows the error.
- **Unknown outcome:** if the connection to the register timed out, check the state of the device, for example via a Zero-Receipt, before the request is sent again.

For the POS system integration, see [Offline Handling (PL)](../../../../../../poscreators/middleware-doc/middleware-pl/cash-register-integration/offline-handling.md).
