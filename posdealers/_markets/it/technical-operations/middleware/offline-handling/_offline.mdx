- **Internet:** RT printers and RT servers are reached over the local network. The IvaServizi SCU and the CloudCashbox need an internet connection.
- **RT device not reachable:** the receipt is not fiscalized. On local installations with an RT server, asynchronous signing can buffer documents while the device is temporarily unreachable; it requires a persistent `ServiceFolder` or `CacheDirectory`. For more information, see [Synchronous vs. asynchronous signing](../../../../../../poscreators/middleware-doc/middleware-it-registratore-telematico/operation-modes/scu/epsonserver.md#synchronous-vs-asynchronous-signing).
