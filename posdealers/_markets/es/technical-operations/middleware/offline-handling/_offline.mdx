- **Internet:** the Middleware runs in the fiskaltrust cloud and transmits every document to the tax authority in real time. Every location needs a reliable internet connection.
- **Outage:** while the POS system cannot reach the Middleware, no numbered invoice can be issued. In exceptional situations, a provisional receipt may be handed to the customer and must be replaced by the official invoice as soon as the connection is back. Contact fiskaltrust for the recommended procedure for outages.
