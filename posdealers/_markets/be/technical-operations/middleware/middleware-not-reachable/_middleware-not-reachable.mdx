- **No offline mode:** while the POS system cannot reach the Middleware, no sale can be registered and no VAT receipt may be issued.
- **Connection:** a locally installed Middleware is reached over the local network, the fiskaltrust.Middleware for Cloud over the internet connection of the location. Plan the connection of the site accordingly.
