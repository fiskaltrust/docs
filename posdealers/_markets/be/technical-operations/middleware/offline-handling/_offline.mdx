- **No offline mode:** sales may only be registered while the FDM is connected and working. The Middleware does not sign receipts later.
- **Internet:** the Middleware reaches the FDM over HTTPS, both in the cloud and when installed locally. If this connection fails, no sales can be registered. Plan the internet connection of the site accordingly.
- **Responsibility:** the distributor or installer is responsible for the correct and secure installation and configuration of the connection to the FDM.

For the POS system integration, see [Offline Handling (BE)](../../../../../../poscreators/middleware-doc/middleware-be/cash-register-integration/offline-handling.md).
